    How Mindful Joy works

    Mindful Joy is a free meditation timer that runs in your web browser. Choose a length, press play, and soft bells mark the start, the end and any moments in between. There is no account to make, and your settings and history stay on your own device.

    How long should I meditate?

    There is no single right length. Many people start with 5 to 10 minutes and add time as it feels comfortable. A short session you do regularly can be worth more than a long one you skip. Pick 5 to 60 minutes from the length menu, or choose a custom length of your own.

    What are interval bells?

    Interval bells are optional bells that ring part-way through a session, for example every 5 minutes, or at times you choose, such as 3 and 7 minutes. They are handy for returning your attention or marking the stages of a longer sit. Open Bell Settings to turn them on and to choose the bell sound, volume and number of strikes.

    What is a sit and walk session?

    Sit + walk lets you build one session from several parts, such as 20 minutes sitting, 10 minutes of walking meditation and 20 minutes sitting again. A bell tells you when it is time to switch. Choose Sit + walk in the length menu, then add, remove and reorder the parts. You can save your favorite layouts.

    What are box, 4-7-8 and coherent breathing?

    The Breathe tab paces your breath with a circle that grows slowly as you breathe in and shrinks as you breathe out. Box breathing uses four equal steps of 4 seconds: breathe in, hold, breathe out, hold. The 4-7-8 pattern breathes in for 4, holds for 7 and breathes out for 8. Coherent breathing is an even 5 seconds in and 5 seconds out. Breathe comfortably, and stop if you feel lightheaded.

    Can I see my progress?

    Yes. Under the More button, History shows your current and best streaks, a chart of the past seven days and a calendar of the last twelve weeks. A day counts toward a streak at five minutes or more. History is kept on your device only. Use Export and Import in the gear menu to move it to another device or keep a backup.

    Does it work offline and on my phone?

    Yes. After your first visit the timers keep working without a connection, and you can add Mindful Joy to your home screen from your browser’s menu. Keep the page open and your volume up during a session. Some phones mute web audio when the ringer switch is off, so check it if you do not hear the bells.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. Mindful Joy is free to use, needs no sign-in, and is open source under the MIT License. It is supported by advertising and by optional contributions through Ko-fi. Read more on the About page.
