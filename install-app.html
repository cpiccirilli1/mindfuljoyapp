How to Install Mindful Joy as an App

A short how-to · Updated November 24, 2026

Mindful Joy is a website, but you can install it so it works like an app: it opens in its own window, starts fast from your home screen or desktop, and keeps working without an internet connection. There is no app store and no account, and it takes about thirty seconds. This guide covers Chrome, Edge and Safari on phones, tablets and computers.

Jump to: Chrome Edge Safari Firefox

Before you start

  • Open mindfuljoy.app in your browser while you are online. The first visit saves everything the app needs.
  • Use a normal browser window, not a private or incognito one. Installing is not offered in private windows.
  • If you already have some history saved, use Export in the gear menu first. An installed app can keep its own separate storage, so your history may not carry over by itself, and a backup file lets you bring it in with Import.

Menu names change a little between browser versions, so if a label looks different on your device, look for the closest match: “Install”, “Add to Home Screen” or “Add to Dock”.

Chrome

Computer (Windows, Mac, Linux, ChromeOS)

  1. Open mindfuljoy.app.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (a small screen with a down arrow). If you do not see it, open the three-dot menu and choose Cast, save and share, then Install page as app (on some versions it reads Install Mindful Joy).
  3. Click Install. The app opens in its own window and is added to your apps or Start menu.

Android

  1. Open mindfuljoy.app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Tap Install. The Mindful Joy icon appears on your home screen.

Edge

Computer

  1. Open mindfuljoy.app.
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar, or open the three-dot menu, choose Apps, then Install this site as an app.
  3. Click Install. You can choose to pin it to the taskbar or Start menu.

Android

  1. Open mindfuljoy.app in Edge.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu, then Add to phone (on some versions, Add to Home screen).
  3. Confirm, and the icon is added to your home screen.

Safari

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open mindfuljoy.app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up). You may need to scroll the list of actions.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen. If you see an Open as Web App switch, leave it on.
  4. Tap Add. The Mindful Joy icon appears on your home screen.

On recent versions of iOS and iPadOS, other browsers such as Chrome can also add sites to the home screen from their Share menu. Safari is the most dependable way if something does not work.

Mac

  1. Open mindfuljoy.app in Safari. This needs macOS Sonoma (14) or later.
  2. Choose File in the menu bar, then Add to Dock.
  3. Click Add. Mindful Joy appears in your Dock and in Launchpad.

What about Firefox?

Firefox on a computer does not support installing websites as apps. On Android, open the Firefox menu and look for Install (or Add to Home screen). If you use Firefox on a computer, Mindful Joy still works perfectly in a tab, and you can keep it as a bookmark, or open it in Chrome, Edge or Safari to install it.

After you install

  • It works offline. The timer, breathing, history and guides all run without a connection. Only ads and the Ko-fi link need the internet.
  • It starts faster. The app opens from files already saved on your device.
  • Updates arrive by themselves. When there is a new version, a small bar offers a Reload, or it appears the next time you open the app.
  • Your history stays on your device. To move it to another device, use Export and Import in the gear menu.

If you do not see an install option

  • Check you are using Chrome, Edge or Safari, in a normal (not private) window.
  • Reload the page once while online, then try again.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the option is in the Share menu, not the address bar.
  • If the app is already installed, the browser may only offer to open it.

Uninstalling

On a phone or tablet, remove the icon the way you remove any app (touch and hold, then remove). On a computer, open the installed app, then use the three-dot or app menu and choose Uninstall, or remove it from your apps list. Removing the app does not affect the website.
