About Mindful Joy

A simple, free meditation timer.

Mindful Joy is a free meditation timer that runs in your web browser. It requires no account and keeps your settings on your own device. The site is supported by advertising and by optional contributions. Choose a length, settle in, and let the bells keep time.

Features

  • Timer: Sessions from 5 to 60 minutes, a custom length of up to four hours, or a sit and walk sequence built from several segments, with its own bell between segments. Options include a preparation delay, start and end bells (one to five strikes each), bell volume, interval bells at repeating or custom times, and an optional vibration (off by default) at the start and end of a session on devices that support it.
  • Breathe: A guided breathing pacer with box breathing, 4-7-8, and an even five-second coherent pattern. The circle grows slowly through the whole inhale and shrinks through the whole exhale, and soft tones mark each step. The tones can be switched off with the Sound menu.
  • Reflect: Short prompts for reflection before or after a session.
  • Articles: Short, practical guides, starting with How to Use Mindful Joy. They are general information, not medical advice.
  • Clear labels: Each timer names itself above the clock (Meditation, Pomodoro, Count Up or Stopwatch), so you always know which one you are using.
  • More Timers: A Pomodoro timer with adjustable focus, break and round lengths, an open-ended Count Up timer with optional interval chimes, and a Stopwatch with hundredths of a second and lap times, all found under the “More” button.
  • Themes: Twenty-five color themes, grouped as Light, Dark, and Pride, selected from the gear icon at the top of the page. Most light themes have a matching dark version, and the High Contrast theme (in the Dark group) uses pure black and white for maximum legibility and efficient display on OLED screens. The two Pride themes draw the timer ring in seven rainbow segments. Sage is the default.
  • History and streaks: Under the “More” button, History shows your current and best streaks, a chart of the past seven days, a twelve-week calendar and your recent sessions. A day counts toward a streak at five minutes or more. History is kept only on your own device.
  • Export and Import: In the gear menu, “Your data” saves your history (and, if you choose, your settings) to a file and loads it on another device. Importing adds to the history you have and never creates duplicates.
  • Saved preferences: Your settings, including bell choices, the breathing sound setting, Pomodoro and Count Up options, and any saved sit and walk layouts, are stored on your own device, so Mindful Joy is ready the way you left it.
  • Accessibility: Keyboard shortcuts (Space to start or pause and R to reset on every timer, B to open Bell Settings on the main timer, and L to record a lap on the Stopwatch), screen reader announcements for session changes, and visible focus indicators.
  • Mobile: Designed for phones, and keeps the screen awake while a timer is running where the browser permits.
  • Offline and installable: After your first visit the timers keep working without a connection, and your browser can add Mindful Joy to your home screen or desktop.

Land Acknowledgement

Mindful Joy is made and used on the homelands of Indigenous peoples, who cared for these lands long before any of us arrived and who are still here today. Much of this land was taken through force, broken treaties, and displacement. We honor Indigenous peoples past and present, and their continuing relationship with the land.

Pausing to learn whose land you are on can be a mindful practice in itself. You can find the Indigenous territories where you live, with Native Land Digital’s interactive map, at native-land.ca.

Modifications. For use in Mindful Joy, the recordings were trimmed, volume-normalized, faded at the end, and converted to mono MP3. They are served as audio files from this site. Beyond these adjustments, the recordings are unaltered.
