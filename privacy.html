Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Mindful Joy does not ask for or store your personal information. The site displays advertising served by Google, which uses cookies and similar technologies as described below.

1. Information We Collect

The timer, breathing pacer, and reflection tools do not collect personal information. There are no accounts or sign-in, and we do not run our own analytics or tracking tools. Advertising and font services provided by third parties may collect certain information when you visit the site, as described in sections 4 and 5.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To save you from re-entering your choices, Mindful Joy stores your preferences in your browser’s local storage. These include session length, the segments of any sit and walk sequence and the layouts you choose to save, bell sound, bell volume, number of bells, interval settings, breathing pattern, whether the breathing tones are on, Pomodoro and Count Up options, whether vibration is on, and color theme. If you accept the cookie notice, that choice is saved here as well. This information remains on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to any third party, and it contains no personal information. You may erase it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings. Private or incognito windows may not retain it.

To let the timers work without a connection, your browser also keeps a saved copy of the site’s own pages, scripts, images and sounds (a service worker cache). This contains no personal information, is not sent anywhere, and is cleared along with the site’s other data.

3. Device Features

  • Audio: Mindful Joy plays bell sounds, and soft tones in the Breathe tool, through your device. It does not access your microphone.
  • Screen wake lock: During a session, Mindful Joy may ask your browser to keep the screen awake. This request is handled entirely on your device and shares no data.

4. Third-Party Services

Loading and using the page involves services operated by others:

  • Google AdSense: This site displays advertisements served by Google. See section 5 for details.
  • Google Fonts: The Inter typeface is loaded from Google. As a result, your browser contacts Google’s servers, and Google receives standard request information such as your IP address. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Page hosting: The page is delivered by Cloudflare, a web hosting and network provider, which may process routine technical information, such as IP addresses, to deliver the page and maintain security, in accordance with its own policies.
  • Ko-fi and other external links: The Donations button and the links on the About page open external websites with their own privacy practices. If you make a contribution through Ko-fi, Ko-fi processes the transaction and your payment details. Mindful Joy does not receive or store them.

5. Advertising and Cookies

This site is supported by advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet. Google and its partners may also use device identifiers and your IP address to deliver and measure ads.

Mindful Joy does not control the information these vendors collect and does not receive it. To learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You may also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info, or block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies does not prevent ads from appearing, but they may be less relevant to you. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland may be shown a consent message before personalized ads are served.

Cookie notice. When you first visit, a banner asks whether you consent to cookies. If you select Accept, your choice is saved on your device so the banner does not appear again. If you select Reject, you are taken to google.com.

6. Children’s Privacy

Mindful Joy is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. The tools themselves collect no personal information. Advertising served by Google may use cookies, as described in section 5.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
