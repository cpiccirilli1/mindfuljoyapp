How to Use Mindful Joy

A quick guide · Updated October 25, 2026

Mindful Joy is a free meditation timer that runs in your web browser. This guide covers what it can do and how to get the most from it, in a few minutes of reading. Nothing here is required: you can press play on the home page and start right away.

Quick start

  1. Choose a length. Pick 5 to 60 minutes from the length menu, or choose a custom length.
  2. Press play. A short countdown gives you a few seconds to settle, then a bell marks the start.
  3. Sit. A bell tells you when the time is up. You do not have to watch the screen.

How long should I meditate?

There is no single right length. Many people start with 5 to 10 minutes and add time as it feels comfortable. A short session you do regularly can be worth more than a long one you skip, so choose a length you can keep. You can change it at any time.

Interval bells

Interval bells are optional bells that ring part-way through a session. You can set them to repeat, for example every 5 minutes, or to ring at times you choose, such as 3 and 7 minutes. They are handy for gently returning your attention or for marking the stages of a longer sit.

Open Bell Settings to turn them on. There you can also choose the bell sound, the volume, and how many times the start and end bells strike. Every bell is a real recording, and the credits are on the About page.

Sit and walk sessions

Some traditions alternate sitting with walking meditation. Choose Sit + walk in the length menu to build one session from several parts, such as 20 minutes sitting, 10 minutes walking and 20 minutes sitting again. A bell tells you when it is time to switch, and the screen shows the current part and what comes next. You can add, remove and reorder the parts, and save the layouts you like to use again.

Box, 4-7-8 and coherent breathing

The Breathe tab paces your breath with a circle. It grows slowly for the whole of each inhale and shrinks slowly for the whole of each exhale, and soft tones mark each step. You can switch the tones off.

  • Box breathing uses four equal steps of 4 seconds: breathe in, hold, breathe out, hold.
  • 4-7-8 breathes in for 4 seconds, holds for 7 and breathes out for 8.
  • Coherent breathing is an even 5 seconds in and 5 seconds out.

Breathe comfortably, and never strain. If you feel lightheaded, stop and breathe normally.

Tracking your progress

Under the More button, History shows your current and best streaks, a chart of the past seven days and a calendar of the last twelve weeks. A day counts toward a streak at five minutes or more, and short sessions on the same day add up. Sessions that end early still count for the time you sat.

Your history is kept on your own device only. To move it to another phone or computer, or to keep a backup, use Export and Import in the gear menu. Importing adds to the history you already have and never creates duplicates.

Other timers

The More menu also holds a Pomodoro timer for focused work with short breaks, an open-ended Count Up timer, and a Stopwatch with lap times. Each timer shows its name above the clock, so it is always clear which one you are using.

Offline, phones and keeping the screen on

After your first visit the timers keep working without a connection, and your browser can add Mindful Joy to your home screen from its menu. While a timer runs, the page asks your browser to keep the screen awake where it can. Keep the page open and your volume up during a session. Some phones mute web audio when the ringer switch is off, so check it if you do not hear the bells.

Keyboard users can press Space to start or pause, R to reset and B to open Bell Settings.

Is it really free?

Yes. Mindful Joy is free to use, needs no account, and is open source under the MIT License. It is supported by advertising and by optional contributions through Ko-fi. You can read how your information is handled in the Privacy Policy.
